🔐 Password & Account Checklist

Good account security starts with unique credentials and additional protection.

  • I use unique passwords for important accounts.
  • I use a trusted password manager when appropriate.
  • I enable multi-factor authentication where available.
  • I do not share passwords or OTPs with others.
  • I review important account security settings.

🎣 Phishing Checklist

Slow down before clicking unexpected links or responding to urgent requests.

  • I check the sender before trusting a message.
  • I inspect links before opening them.
  • I avoid urgent payment or verification requests.
  • I verify unexpected requests using another trusted channel.
  • I never enter sensitive information on suspicious websites.

📱 Mobile Safety Checklist

Keep your phone, applications and personal data protected.

  • I keep my phone software updated.
  • I install apps from trusted sources.
  • I review app permissions regularly.
  • I use a screen lock on my device.
  • I avoid unknown remote-access applications.

💳 Payment Safety Checklist

Use extra caution whenever money or financial information is involved.

  • I verify the recipient before making a payment.
  • I never share my UPI PIN or OTP.
  • I verify payment requests independently.
  • I check transaction notifications carefully.
  • I contact my bank quickly if I notice an unauthorized transaction.
