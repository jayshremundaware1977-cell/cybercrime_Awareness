CYBER SAFETY & PREVENTION

Make Safety a Daily Habit

Simple digital habits can reduce many everyday cyber risks. Use these practical checklists to protect your accounts, devices, payments and personal information.

Practical Protection

Cyber Safety Checklist

Focus on the areas you use every day instead of trying to remember complicated security rules.

🔐

Password & Accounts

Protect your digital identity

Strong account security reduces the risk of unauthorized access.

  • ✓ Use a unique password for important accounts.
  • ✓ Use a password manager where appropriate.
  • ✓ Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • ✓ Never share passwords or verification codes.
  • ✓ Review account recovery options regularly.
📱

Mobile & Device Safety

Keep devices protected

Your phone and computer contain valuable personal information.

  • ✓ Keep the operating system updated.
  • ✓ Install apps from trusted sources.
  • ✓ Use a screen lock or biometric protection.
  • ✓ Remove apps you no longer need.
  • ✓ Back up important information regularly.
💳

Online Payment Safety

Think before you pay

Digital payments are convenient, but unexpected requests deserve careful verification.

  • ✓ Verify the recipient before sending money.
  • ✓ Never share your UPI PIN or OTP.
  • ✓ Do not scan unknown QR codes to receive money.
  • ✓ Check transaction details before confirming.
  • ✓ Use official banking/payment applications.
📲

Social Media Safety

Protect your online presence

Public information can sometimes be used for impersonation or social engineering.

  • ✓ Review privacy settings regularly.
  • ✓ Be careful with unknown friend/follow requests.
  • ✓ Avoid posting sensitive personal details.
  • ✓ Use MFA on social accounts.
  • ✓ Report suspicious or impersonating profiles.
📧

Email Safety

Stop phishing before it starts

Unexpected email should be treated carefully, especially when it asks you to click, download or pay.

  • ✓ Check the sender address carefully.
  • ✓ Be cautious with unexpected attachments.
  • ✓ Verify links before opening them.
  • ✓ Do not reply with sensitive information.
  • ✓ Report suspicious messages through available tools.
📶

Public Wi-Fi

Use public networks carefully

Public networks may not provide the same level of trust as your personal network.

  • ✓ Confirm the correct network name.
  • ✓ Avoid sensitive activity on untrusted networks.
  • ✓ Keep device security features enabled.
  • ✓ Disable automatic connection when appropriate.
  • ✓ Forget networks you no longer use.
🤖

AI & Deepfake Safety

Verify before trusting

AI can make fake audio, images and video more convincing. Verification is increasingly important.

  • ✓ Do not trust audio/video alone for urgent requests.
  • ✓ Verify unusual requests through another channel.
  • ✓ Check context and source of important content.
  • ✓ Avoid forwarding unverified claims.
  • ✓ Be careful with sensitive information shared with AI tools.
👨‍👩‍👧

Family & Children

Build safe digital habits

Children and families can benefit from simple conversations about privacy, strangers and online pressure.

  • ✓ Teach children not to share passwords or OTPs.
  • ✓ Encourage them to ask before clicking suspicious links.
  • ✓ Discuss privacy and responsible sharing.
  • ✓ Use appropriate parental controls when needed.
  • ✓ Encourage reporting of uncomfortable online interactions.
Before You Click

The 5-Second Safety Check

When a message creates pressure, pause and check these five things.

🔎 Ask Yourself

1

Do I know who actually sent this?

2

Was I expecting this message, call or request?

3

Is someone creating unnecessary urgency?

4

Am I being asked for money, OTP, PIN or password?

5

Can I verify this through an official channel?

🛡️ Safer Response

1

Pause instead of responding immediately.

2

Do not click an unexpected link.

3

Find the organization's official website or app yourself.

4

Verify the request using a separate trusted channel.

5

If suspicious, report it instead of forwarding it.

Remember

Do's & Don'ts

Keep these simple rules in mind while using digital services.

✓ Do

  • Use multi-factor authentication.
  • Keep devices and apps updated.
  • Verify unexpected requests.
  • Use official websites and applications.
  • Keep backups of important data.
  • Report suspicious activity.

✕ Don't

  • Don't share OTPs, PINs or passwords.
  • Don't click unexpected links blindly.
  • Don't install unknown applications.
  • Don't send money because of pressure or fear.
  • Don't assume a caller is genuine because they know your name.
  • Don't forward unverified information.
🚨

Think You Have Been Scammed?

Stop further interaction, preserve relevant evidence and use appropriate official channels to seek help or report the incident.
