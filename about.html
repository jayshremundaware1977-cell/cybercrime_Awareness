ABOUT CYBERSAFE

Building a Safer Digital Community

CyberSafe is an awareness and prevention initiative designed to help people understand common cyber threats, develop safer online habits and know where to seek help when something goes wrong.

Who We Are

About the Workshop

The CyberSafe Cybercrime Awareness & Prevention Workshop focuses on practical digital safety education.

The workshop introduces participants to common forms of cybercrime such as phishing, online payment fraud, identity theft, social engineering, malware and impersonation scams.

Instead of only learning definitions, participants are encouraged to recognize warning signs, verify information, protect their accounts and understand appropriate steps after a cyber incident.

🛡️

Our Approach

Learn → Check → Prevent → Report


We combine awareness, practical examples, interactive activities and safety guidance to make cyber education easier to understand and apply in everyday digital life.

Our Objectives

What We Want to Achieve

The workshop is designed around practical cyber-safety awareness rather than technical knowledge alone.

👁️

Increase Awareness

Help participants recognize common cyber threats, scams and suspicious online behavior.

🛡️

Promote Prevention

Encourage safer habits for passwords, payments, devices, email and social media.

🧠

Build Digital Skills

Teach practical methods for checking information before clicking, sharing or paying.

🚨

Improve Response

Help participants understand what actions to consider after becoming a victim of cybercrime.

🔎

Encourage Verification

Promote independent verification of unexpected messages, calls, links and requests.

🤝

Build Responsibility

Encourage responsible and informed participation in the digital environment.

Learning Outcomes

After the Workshop

Participants should leave with practical knowledge they can apply to their everyday digital activities.

  • ✓ Recognize common phishing and scam indicators.
  • ✓ Understand safer online payment practices.
  • ✓ Improve password and account security habits.
  • ✓ Identify suspicious messages and links.
  • ✓ Understand social engineering techniques.
  • ✓ Protect personal information and online identity.
  • ✓ Know what information to preserve after an incident.
  • ✓ Find appropriate official reporting resources.
