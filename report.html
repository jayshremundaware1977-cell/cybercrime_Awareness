CYBERCRIME REPORTING GUIDE

Don't Panic.
Know What to Do.

If you encounter an online scam, suspicious transaction, account compromise or another cyber incident, use this guide to understand the next steps.

🚨 If Money Was Lost

Act quickly. Contact your bank or payment service through its official customer-support channel and use the official cybercrime reporting channel available in your country. Do not wait for the scammer to respond.

Identify the Incident

What Happened?

Different incidents may require different immediate actions.

💳

Online Payment Fraud

Unauthorized payment, UPI scam, card fraud or suspicious money transfer.

🎣

Phishing

Fake email, SMS, website or message asking for credentials or payment.

👤

Account Compromise

Someone may have accessed your email, social media or another online account.

📱

Mobile / App Scam

Suspicious application, remote-access request or unexpected device activity.

🪪

Identity Misuse

Personal information appears to have been misused or exposed without authorization.

🗣️

Impersonation / Scam

Someone is pretending to be a bank, company, friend, official or another person.

Immediate Response

Follow These Steps

These general steps can help reduce further damage and preserve useful information.

1

Stop

Stop communicating with the suspected scammer. Do not send additional money, OTPs or passwords.

2

Secure

Change compromised passwords, enable MFA and contact your bank or service provider if needed.

3

Preserve

Keep transaction details, messages, screenshots, email headers and relevant URLs.

4

Report

Use an appropriate official reporting channel and provide accurate information.

Official Channels

Where Can You Report?

Use official government or service-provider channels instead of links received from unknown people.

🇮🇳 India — Cybercrime Reporting
