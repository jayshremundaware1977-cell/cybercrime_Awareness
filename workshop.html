Workshop Curriculum

Topics We Cover

Sessions focus on practical awareness and everyday digital safety.

🎣

Phishing & Online Scams

Understand fake messages, suspicious links, impersonation attempts and common scam patterns.

💳

Digital Payment Safety

Learn safer practices for online payments, UPI, QR codes and unexpected payment requests.

🔐

Password & Account Security

Learn about strong passwords, password managers and multi-factor authentication.

📱

Mobile & App Safety

Understand app permissions, updates, suspicious applications and device protection.

🧠

Social Engineering

Learn how manipulation, urgency and impersonation can influence online decisions.

🤖

AI & Deepfake Awareness

Understand why audio, images and videos may need independent verification.

👤

Identity & Privacy

Learn practical ways to protect personal information and online identity.

🚨

Incident Response

Understand what to do after a suspicious transaction, account compromise or cyber incident.

📢

Cybercrime Reporting

Learn how to preserve relevant information and find appropriate official reporting channels.
