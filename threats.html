Understand The Risk

Common Cyber Threats

Scams can look different, but many use urgency, fear, impersonation or attractive offers to influence decisions.

🎣

Phishing

Fake messages & websites

Phishing attempts try to trick people into opening malicious links or providing passwords, financial information or other sensitive details.

⚠️ Warning Signs

Urgent language, unexpected links, unusual sender addresses, requests for passwords or OTPs, and unfamiliar websites.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Verify the sender independently. Avoid entering sensitive information through unexpected links.

💳

Payment Fraud

Digital payment scams

Fraudsters may use fake payment requests, impersonation, QR-code tricks or other social-engineering methods to influence a victim into making or authorizing a payment.

⚠️ Warning Signs

Pressure to pay immediately, unexpected payment requests, unknown QR codes and requests to share PINs or OTPs.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Verify payment requests through a trusted channel and never share UPI PINs, passwords or OTPs.

🏦

KYC Scam

Fake verification requests

A scammer may impersonate a bank, service provider or other organization and claim that an account will be blocked unless KYC or verification is completed immediately.

⚠️ Warning Signs

Threats of account closure, requests for OTPs or PINs, unknown APK/app installations and suspicious links.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Contact the organization using its official website, app or known customer-service channel.

💼

Job Scams

Fake employment offers

Fake job offers may promise easy income and then ask applicants for registration fees, deposits, personal information or access to accounts.

⚠️ Warning Signs

Guaranteed income, payment before employment, pressure to act quickly and communication only through unofficial channels.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Research the employer independently and verify the vacancy through an official company website.

👤

Identity Theft

Misuse of personal information

Identity theft involves unauthorized use of another person's personal information, credentials or identity-related data.

⚠️ Warning Signs

Unknown account activity, unexpected login alerts, unfamiliar transactions or services you did not request.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Limit unnecessary sharing of personal information and use strong, unique credentials with multi-factor authentication.

🦠

Malware

Harmful software

Malware is software designed to perform harmful or unauthorized actions on a device, such as stealing information or disrupting normal operation.

⚠️ Warning Signs

Unknown applications, unusual device behavior, unexpected pop-ups and suspicious downloads.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Keep software updated, use trusted app sources and avoid opening unexpected attachments or downloads.

🧠

Social Engineering

Manipulation & impersonation

Social engineering uses psychological manipulation to convince people to reveal information, transfer money or perform an unsafe action.

⚠️ Warning Signs

Fear, urgency, authority claims, secrecy and requests to bypass normal verification procedures.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Slow down and verify unusual requests independently, especially when money or sensitive information is involved.

🤖

AI & Deepfakes

Manipulated digital content

AI-generated or manipulated audio, images and video can be used to impersonate people or create misleading content.

⚠️ Warning Signs

Unexpected emergency requests, unusual audio/video, inconsistent details and requests that cannot be independently verified.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Verify important requests using a separate trusted channel instead of relying only on a voice, image or video.

Think Before You Act

Common Red Flags

A single sign does not prove that something is fraudulent, but several warning signs together deserve extra caution.

⏰

Urgency

“Act now” or “your account will be blocked” messages are designed to create pressure.

🔑

Secret Information

Requests for OTPs, PINs, passwords or recovery codes should receive careful attention.

🔗

Unknown Links

Unexpected links can lead to fake websites or harmful downloads.

💰

Unexpected Money Request

Verify unexpected requests for money through a trusted, independent channel.
